How it works. Once a day we collect articles and NGO reports from outlets worldwide. Each must pass strict matching checks before it appears: the company or an approved brand name has to feature in the headline or opening paragraphs, the story has to relate to a tracked topic, and an automated check confirms it is substantively about the company. Borderline items are held back for human review. Stories stay up while coverage continues, then expire after a period without updates.

Headlines, snippets and images belong to their publishers, and this site links to the original articles. Summaries, tags and relevance checks are generated automatically by AI and can be wrong, so always check the original reports.
